    AI-First OER Panel Interest Venn¶

    Run the AI-First OER Panel Venn MicroSim Fullscreen Send Feedback

    You can add this MicroSim to any web page with this iframe:

    <iframe src="https://dmccreary.github.io/oer-evaluator/sims/ai-first-oer-panel-venn/main.html"
        height="692px"
        width="100%"
        scrolling="no"></iframe>

    Description¶

    Delmar Larsen has proposed a 90-minute online panel called "What would an AI-first OER infrastructure look like?" The panelists are Dan McCreary, David Wiley, and Joel Gladd, and Delmar is the host. The date has not been set, and it has not yet been decided whether the panel will be public.

    This MicroSim draws one translucent circle for each person. Each label sits in the region for the people who share that interest. Gold labels are interests that all four people share.

    The interests are a first draft drawn from public pages and the panel invitation. They may be incomplete. If an interest is wrong or missing, use the Send Feedback button.

    How to Use¶

    • Hover a label to see, in the bar at the bottom, the names of everyone who shares that interest.
    • Read from the center out. The center holds what everyone shares. The regions where two circles meet hold interests shared by two people, and the outer parts hold interests unique to one person.
    • The diagram resizes to fit the width of the window.

    Lesson Plan¶

    Learning Objective¶

    After using this MicroSim, the learner can analyze where the four speakers' interests overlap and write one question for the panel that draws on an interest shared by at least two speakers. (Bloom level: Analyze.)

    Audience¶

    Adult learners: OER coordinators, faculty, instructional designers, and anyone planning to attend the panel.

    Duration¶

    10 minutes.

    Activities¶

    1. Explore (3 min). Hover every gold label and every label where two circles meet.
    2. Question (5 min). Write one question for the panel that draws on a shared interest, such as "How could LibreTexts authoring tools support AI literacy?"
    3. Predict (2 min). For your question, predict which speaker will answer first and why.

    Prompt for Generating a Lesson Plan¶

    Using the AI-First OER Panel Interest Venn MicroSim, create a 30-minute pre-panel activity for community college faculty. Learners identify the shared interests of the speakers, write questions about AI-first OER infrastructure, and rank their questions by how many speakers can address each one.
