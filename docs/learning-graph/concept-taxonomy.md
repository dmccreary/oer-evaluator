# Concept Taxonomy

Every concept in the [Concept List](concept-list.md) belongs to one of these 14 categories. The TaxonomyID is the abbreviation used in `learning-graph.csv` and in the graph viewer legend.

| Category | TaxonomyID | Concept IDs | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| OER Foundations | OERF | 1 to 21 | Core ideas: what an open educational resource is, the 5R permissions, and adopting, adapting, and creating OER. |
| Open Licensing | LIC | 22 to 44 | Open licenses, the Creative Commons family and its elements, attribution, compatibility, and choosing a license. |
| Rubric and Scoring | RUB | 45 to 69, 352 | The 10-metric rubric: dimensions, scores, evidence, subtotals, reviewers, and rubric reliability. |
| Content Quality Metrics | CQM | 70 to 109 | The accuracy and currency, alignment, and readability metrics and how to score them. |
| Accessibility and Practice Metrics | APM | 110 to 149 | The accessibility metric (WCAG) and the practice-with-feedback metric, including quizzes and simulations. |
| Modularity and Metadata | MDM | 150 to 172 | The open license, modularity, and metadata metrics: standalone components, Dublin Core, and metadata completeness. |
| Editable Source and Portability | SRP | 173 to 197 | The editable source and portability metrics: open file formats, version control, embedding, and LMS import. |
| Manual Evaluation | MANU | 198 to 208 | Scoring an OER by hand: recording evidence, calibration, consensus, and the adoption decision. |
| AI Agents and Prompts | AIA | 209 to 239 | AI assistants, agents, skills, prompts, structured output, automated checks, cost, and student data privacy. |
| Checking Agent Output | CHK | 240 to 261 | Hallucination and other agent errors, verification, and measuring agreement with human reviewers. |
| Improving Resources | IMP | 262 to 293 | Improvement plans, AI-assisted fixes for each metric, re-scoring, derivative works, and reusable components. |
| A/B Testing with Students | ABT | 294 to 322 | Designing and running an A/B test with students and analyzing the results. |
| Publishing and Sharing | PUB | 323 to 335 | Publishing improved OER, hosting, repositories, maintenance, community contributions, and impact reporting. |
| Training and Certification | TRN | 336 to 351 | Teacher training workshops, the certificate, peer review, local reviewers, and the capstone project. |
