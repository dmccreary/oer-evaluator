# Character Sheet: Echo the Loon

The canonical identity document for Echo, the pedagogical mascot for the
**OER Evaluator** textbook. Every pose prompt and every piece of AI-generated
content involving this character must re-anchor to the description below. It
is the source of truth for visual and voice consistency.

## Identity

- **Name:** Echo
- **Species:** Loon
- **Subject:** Evaluating and improving open educational resources (OER)
- **Catchphrase:** "Let's dive in and check the evidence!"
- **Pronouns:** Echo has no gender. Write "Echo" or *they/them*.

## Visual Description

- **Body color:** Glossy near-black head and neck with a white belly and a black back covered in a white checkered pattern, as on a common loon. Hex `#1a1a2e` for the black.
- **Accent color:** Indigo, hex `#3949ab`, with a burnt-orange accent, hex `#b45309`. These match the book's indigo and orange theme.
- **Clothing / accessories:** A small indigo scarf, and a magnifying glass with an orange handle held in one wing. Echo also has the loon's red eye.
- **Expression:** Calm, sharp-eyed, and friendly, with a small closed-beak smile.
- **Size proportion:** Small and compact, suitable for icon-sized display at about 90 pixels.
- **Art style:** Flat cartoon illustration, modern vector, clean lines.

## Personality

- Patient: Echo never rushes a reviewer or a learner.
- Sharp-eyed: Echo notices the detail others skip, such as a missing license or a broken link.
- Encouraging: Echo treats mistakes as part of the process and celebrates progress.
- Gently skeptical: Echo checks before trusting, especially an AI agent's confident answer.

## Voice

- Short, calm sentences that name the specific thing to check.
- Asks "What is the evidence?" before accepting a claim, and models checking the work.
- Uses water words sparingly and only when they fit: dive in, below the surface, ripples, calling across the lake. Never more than one per admonition.
- Signature phrases: "Let's dive in and check the evidence!", "Look below the surface.", "Credit it, share it, pass it on."

## Why This Mascot

A loon dives beneath the surface to see what is really there, which is what
this book asks teachers to do when they check evidence and verify what an AI
agent reports. A loon's call carries across a whole lake, which fits the idea
of sharing and reusing OER, and the name Echo says the same thing. The loon
is also Minnesota's state bird, which ties the book to the Minneapolis
workshop it supports.
