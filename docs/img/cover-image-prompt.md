# Cover Image Prompt

Please generate a professional-quality cover image for this textbook.
This image will be used in social media previews and must follow the
formatting guidelines for an Open Graph image preview.

**Required specifications:**
- Format: PNG
- Wide-landscape format
- Size: 1200x630 pixels (1.91:1 aspect ratio)
- This is the Open Graph standard for social media previews

The image has four layers, back to front: background montage, color
treatment, mascot, and title text.

## Subject & Tone

OER Evaluator is a textbook about how to judge whether an open educational
resource (OER) is good and whether it can be reused, using a 10-metric rubric
(five quality metrics and five reusability metrics, 100 points in total).
Readers learn to score a resource by hand, direct an AI agent to make the
first pass, check the agent's evidence, improve the resource, and test the
change with students in a manual A/B test. The intended audience is teachers
(K-12 and college), instructional designers, librarians, and OER
coordinators. The visual tone should be clear, trustworthy, and quietly
confident, like a well-made review report: technical enough to signal rigor,
warm enough to feel welcoming to a teacher who has never used an AI agent.
It should look careful and evidence-driven, not flashy.

## Title

Place the words "OER Evaluator" in the center of the image, in a clean,
highly legible sans-serif font, in two or three large, bold words on one
line. Use a white font color with a subtle drop shadow or a dark indigo
scrim behind it so it stays readable against the montage background. Keep
the title large — do not shrink it to fit; instead simplify the background
directly behind the text. Render only the title as readable text. Do not add
a subtitle, tagline, author name, or any other sentence.

## Background Montage

Arrange a montage of the following 8 concepts around the title, each
rendered in a consistent illustration style (see Style below) so the
composition reads as one image rather than a collage of unrelated styles.
Each element is a small, self-contained "card" or "badge" with softly
rounded corners and a thin light outline, drawn in the same flat style:

1. **Six Creative Commons licenses** (top left) — a 3x2 grid of six small
   rounded tiles in six soft pastel colors (blue, green, amber, purple,
   teal, rose). Each tile shows simplified license icons in black circles: a
   person, a circle with a slash, a circular arrow, an equals sign. The only
   text allowed is the short labels "CC BY", "CC BY-SA", and similar,
   rendered small and crisp. Do not attempt to reproduce the official
   Creative Commons logo exactly; use simplified circle icons.
2. **The ten-metric rubric** (top center, above the title) — a horizontal
   tree diagram. A dark navy root node on the left splits into a blue
   "Quality" node and a purple "Reusability" node, and each of those splits
   into five small pale pills (ten pills in all), like a mind map. No
   readable text on the pills; use thin placeholder lines instead.
3. **The 5R permissions** (top right) — a row of five small circular icons,
   each in its own accent color: a downward arrow into a tray (retain), a
   looping arrow (reuse), a pencil (revise), two overlapping blended
   shapes (remix), and a document with arrows fanning out (redistribute).
4. **Quality and reusability quadrant** (right side, middle) — a square
   2x2 chart with a purple vertical axis, a blue horizontal axis, and four
   differently tinted quadrants. A single bright orange dot sits in the
   upper-right quadrant.
5. **Total score calculator** (bottom right) — three stacked horizontal
   progress bars (blue, purple, and green) with small tick marks, and a
   glowing yellow rounded badge beneath them showing the number "100" or
   "75". The numbers are the only text.
6. **Two reviewers' scores agreeing** (left side, middle) — two vertical
   columns of ten small number chips side by side, with thin lines
   connecting matching chips. Most chips match, and one pair is outlined in
   orange to show a disagreement worth discussing.
7. **An AI agent checking evidence** (lower center, to the right of the
   mascot, below the title) — a friendly, simple robot head or chat-bubble
   agent beside a document. A highlighted passage in the document is
   connected by a thin line to a small green check mark, and one other
   passage is flagged with an orange question mark to show an unverified
   claim. Do not use any real company's logo or brand mark.
8. **A/B test with students** (bottom, far right of center) — two small
   groups of faceless, flat, rounded student silhouettes. Each group holds
   up a book. One book is labeled "A" and drawn plain, and the other is
   labeled "B" and drawn brighter with a small sparkle, joined by a small
   balance-scale or comparison arrow between them.

## Mascot

Place the book's mascot, Echo the Loon, in the lower-left corner, sized so
it does not overlap the title text, and keep it clear of montage element 6.
Echo is a common loon with a glossy near-black head and neck, a white belly,
and a black back covered in a white checkered pattern, with the loon's red
eye. Echo wears a small indigo scarf and holds a magnifying glass with a
burnt-orange handle in one wing, and is waving cheerfully with the other
wing. Echo has a calm, sharp-eyed, friendly expression with a small
closed-beak smile. Echo is drawn in the same flat cartoon vector style as
the rest of the image. Because Echo's head is near black, place a soft pale
glow or a ring of light-blue water ripples behind Echo so the bird stays
clearly separated from the dark indigo background. See the attached
reference image (`docs/img/mascot/welcome.png`) for the exact look of the
character.

## Style & Composition

- Illustration style: flat vector with clean lines and soft rounded corners.
  Apply this one style to every montage element and to Echo. Do not mix in
  3D renders, photographs, or painterly textures.
- Color palette: deep indigo (#3949ab fading to a darker navy, #1a1a2e)
  as the dominant background, with burnt-orange (#b45309) and warm amber
  accents, and pale pastel fills inside the montage cards. The quality
  elements lean blue and the reusability elements lean purple, matching
  the book's rubric.
- Lighting/mood: bright, optimistic, and calm. A subtle set of faint
  concentric water ripples spreads out from behind the title, a quiet nod to
  the loon mascot's habitat and to checking below the surface.
- Composition: title centered, the eight montage cards arranged in a loose
  ring around it at slightly different sizes, Echo in the lower-left,
  generous empty indigo space immediately behind the title so it stays
  readable. Montage cards should be slightly dimmed or softened where they
  are close to the title.

## Avoid

- Do not render dense paragraphs of illegible text anywhere in the image.
  Any text inside a montage element must be a short label that renders
  crisply at small size, or be replaced by thin placeholder lines.
- Avoid generic stock-photo cliches (handshakes, isolated lightbulbs, people
  pointing at whiteboards, stacks of generic books with a graduation cap).
- Avoid photorealistic human faces. Students must be faceless flat
  silhouettes.
- Do not use any real company, AI vendor, or publisher logo, and do not
  reproduce the official Creative Commons logo exactly.
- Do not let montage elements visually compete with or overlap the title.
- Do not add a subtitle, tagline, byline, or any extra words beyond the
  title and the short labels listed above.
