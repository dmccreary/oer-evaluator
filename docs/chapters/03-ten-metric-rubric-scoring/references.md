# References: The Ten-Metric Rubric and Scoring

1. [Rubric (academic)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rubric_(academic)) - Wikipedia - Defines rubrics, their criteria and performance levels, and the difference between analytic and holistic forms. Background for the ten-metric rubric and for how its scoring scales are built.

2. [Inter-rater reliability](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inter-rater_reliability) - Wikipedia - Explains how agreement between independent raters is measured and why it matters for any scoring instrument. Supports the chapter's section on whether two reviewers give the same score to the same resource.

3. [Validity (statistics)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Validity_(statistics)) - Wikipedia - Explains what it means for a measure to capture what it claims to, and outlines the main kinds of validity. Grounds the chapter's question of whether the rubric's total score can be trusted.

4. Introduction to Rubrics: An Assessment Tool to Save Grading Time, Convey Effective Feedback, and Promote Student Learning (2nd ed.) - Dannelle D. Stevens and Antonia J. Levi - Stylus Publishing (2013) - Stevens and Levi break every rubric into four parts: a task description, a scale, the dimensions, and a description of each dimension at each level. That simple grid underlies the metric tables in this book.

5. How to Create and Use Rubrics for Formative Assessment and Grading - Susan M. Brookhart - ASCD (2013) - Brookhart insists that performance-level descriptions describe the work rather than merely label it "good" or "poor." Her advice is the model for writing score descriptions that point to observable evidence.

6. [Achieve OER Rubrics](https://www.achieve.org/node/496) - Achieve - Describes eight rubrics for judging how well an OER aligns to standards and meets other quality criteria, delivered through an online evaluation tool on OER Commons. An earlier example of scoring an OER criterion by criterion.

7. [Not All Rubrics Are Equal: A Review of Rubrics for Evaluating the Quality of Open Educational Resources](https://www.irrodl.org/index.php/irrodl/article/view/2389) - IRRODL - Yuan and Recker's peer-reviewed comparison of fourteen OER quality rubrics shows what each one emphasizes. Useful for seeing where this book's ten metrics overlap with, and differ from, earlier tools.

8. [Creating and Using Rubrics](https://www.cmu.edu/teaching/assessment/assesslearning/rubrics.html) - Carnegie Mellon Eberly Center - Defines the parts of a rubric (criteria, descriptors, and performance levels), lists the benefits for instructors and students, and links to sample rubrics. A short orientation before studying the ten-metric rubric.

9. [QM Rubrics & Standards](https://www.qualitymatters.org/qa-resources/rubric-standards) - Quality Matters - A widely used course-design rubric whose specific review standards carry point values and annotations and are applied by trained peer reviewers. A mature example of an evidence-based rubric used by certified reviewers.

10. [VALUE Rubrics](https://www.aacu.org/value/rubrics) - AAC&U - Faculty-built rubrics with level-by-level descriptors for outcomes such as written communication and critical thinking. A model for writing score descriptions that different reviewers can apply consistently.
