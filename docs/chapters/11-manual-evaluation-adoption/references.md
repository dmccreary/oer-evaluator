# References: Manual Evaluation and Adoption Decisions

1. [Consensus decision-making](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consensus_decision-making) - Wikipedia - Describes how groups reach decisions everyone can accept, and the typical steps and pitfalls. Background for the chapter's methods for reaching agreement after reviewers' scores differ.

2. [Delphi method](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delphi_method) - Wikipedia - Explains the structured process in which experts give independent estimates, see a summary, and revise over rounds. A model for how reviewers can converge without one voice dominating.

3. [Checklist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Checklist) - Wikipedia - Surveys how checklists help people avoid missed steps in fields such as aviation and medicine. Supports the chapter's use of a worksheet and evidence log during manual evaluation.

4. The Checklist Manifesto: How to Get Things Right - Atul Gawande - Metropolitan Books (2009) - Gawande shows through surgery and aviation how a short, well-designed checklist catches errors that experts skip. His account supports the structured worksheet and evidence log used in manual reviews.

5. Noise: A Flaw in Human Judgment - Daniel Kahneman, Olivier Sibony, and Cass R. Sunstein - Little, Brown Spark (2021) - The authors show that independent judges often give very different answers to the same case, and recommend scoring each criterion separately before discussing. This is the logic of scoring first, then reconciling.

6. [Adopt OER](https://www.cccoer.org/using-oer/adopt-oer/) - CCCOER - A five-step guide for instructors: review materials, modify if needed, attribute, obtain curriculum approval, and deliver to students. Matches the path from evaluation to an adoption decision.

7. [The OER Starter Kit](https://iastate.pressbooks.pub/oerstarterkit/) - Iowa State University Digital Press - An open introduction to finding, using, and creating OER, written for faculty and librarians. Useful background for the adopt, revise, or reject decision.

8. [Open Textbook Library](https://open.umn.edu/opentextbooks) - Center for Open Education, University of Minnesota - A catalog of openly licensed textbooks, many with faculty reviews. Shows what published peer reviews look like and offers practice resources for manual scoring.

9. [OER Commons](https://oercommons.org/) - ISKME - A large digital library of open resources whose Evaluate Resource tool applies the Achieve OER rubrics. A place to find candidate resources and see another reviewing workflow.

10. [OpenStax Faculty Resources](https://openstax.org/faculty-resources) - OpenStax - Instructor materials and adoption support for a mature OER publisher. Gives a benchmark for the supporting materials a reviewer might expect when judging a resource ready to adopt.
