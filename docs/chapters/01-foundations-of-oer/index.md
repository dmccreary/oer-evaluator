# Foundations of Open Educational Resources

## Summary

Defines open educational resources and the 5R permissions, and explains adoption, adaptation, and creation, giving teachers the shared vocabulary needed to select and judge open materials. It is the starting point for the book, and every later chapter relies on its vocabulary. After this chapter you can explain what makes a resource open, name the 5R permissions, and describe how teachers adopt, adapt, and create OER.
