# References: Training Teachers and Certifying Reviewers

1. [Professional certification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professional_certification) - Wikipedia - Explains how certifications are awarded after training and assessment, and what makes them credible. Background for the chapter's certificate requirements and submission review.

2. [Capstone course](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capstone_course) - Wikipedia - Describes capstone experiences in which learners apply what they learned to a substantial project. Supports the chapter's capstone project for improving a real OER.

3. [Community of practice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Community_of_practice) - Wikipedia - Explains groups of people who learn together by sharing a practice. Frames how local reviewers and trained colleagues keep improving as a network.

4. Situated Learning: Legitimate Peripheral Participation - Jean Lave and Etienne Wenger - Cambridge University Press (1991) - Introduced legitimate peripheral participation: newcomers become skilled by doing real tasks at the edge of a community's work while experts guide them. The model behind the local reviewer role.

5. Evaluating Professional Development - Thomas R. Guskey - Corwin Press (2000) - Guskey's five levels (participants' reactions, learning, organization support, use of new knowledge, and student outcomes) test whether a workshop changed practice, not only whether people enjoyed it.

6. [CC Training Center](https://creativecommons.org/cc-training-center) - Creative Commons - Offers courses on copyright and open licensing, including the ten-week CC Certificate and a newer badge-based pathway. A working example of a certificate for open licensing skills.

7. [Quality Matters](https://www.qualitymatters.org/) - Quality Matters - A nonprofit that trains peer reviewers and certifies courses against a published rubric. A mature model for training and certifying reviewers, like the local reviewer role in this chapter.

8. [Open Education Leadership Program](https://sparcopen.org/our-work/open-education-leadership-program/) - SPARC - A cohort program that prepares academic leaders to advance open education on their campuses. Shows one way to structure professional development in OER.

9. [Introduction to Communities of Practice](https://wenger-trayner.com/introduction-to-communities-of-practice/) - Wenger-Trayner - Etienne Wenger-Trayner's short introduction to what communities of practice are and how they support learning. Background for building a network of local reviewers.

10. [Open Badges](https://www.1edtech.org/standards/open-badges) - 1EdTech - The standard for verifiable digital credentials that can carry evidence of what the earner did. A model for a certificate that links to a learner's reviewed work.
