# Sample OER Evaluator Workshop Resources

These sample materials support a one-day workshop that trains teachers to
evaluate open educational resources (OER) for quality and reusability with AI
agents, and then to use those agents to improve an OER they already teach
with. Teachers who finish the follow-up work earn the **Certificate of OER
Quality Evaluation Using AI Agents**.

- [Sample One-Day Workshop Schedule](sample-one-day-schedule.md): the 8:30 a.m.
  to 3:30 p.m. agenda for about 20 teachers, with what each session produces
  and what to do before and after the day.
- [Sample Rubric Worksheet](sample-rubric-worksheet.md): a fill-in worksheet
  that scores one OER on five quality and five reusability metrics, compares
  teacher and AI-agent scores, logs agent findings, and lists the certificate
  requirements.
- [Grant-Period Timeline](grant-period-timeline.md): a sample November 2026 to
  May 2027 timeline, from building the rubric and recruiting teachers through
  the training day, certificate work, classroom A/B tests, and the final
  report.
