# Sample Rubric Worksheet

This worksheet scores one open educational resource (OER) out of 100 points:
50 for quality and 50 for reusability, with 10 points per metric. Score each
metric yourself, then record the AI agent's score beside yours so you can see
where you and the agent disagree.

The reusability metrics follow the 5R permissions of open content: the right
to retain, reuse, revise, remix, and redistribute. These are sample metrics;
they will be aligned with the chapters of this book as they are written.

Use it with the [Sample One-Day Workshop Schedule](sample-one-day-schedule.md).

## Certificate Requirements

To earn the **Certificate of OER Quality Evaluation Using AI Agents**, a
teacher:

1. Attends the full training day.
2. Completes the calibration exercise and explains every metric where their
   score and the agent's differ by more than 3 points.
3. Submits an evaluation of one OER from their own subject, scored on all 10
   metrics, with evidence for each score and a note on every agent finding
   they accepted, corrected, or rejected.
4. Publishes one improved component under a CC BY license, with metadata,
   that gains on at least two quality and two reusability metrics.
5. Submits both within 30 days of the workshop. A reviewer checks each
   submission against this rubric.
