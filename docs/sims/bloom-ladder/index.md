## Description

The alignment metric, the outcome verb checks, the quiz bank, and this book's own learning outcomes all use Bloom's levels, so this poster is a reference to come back to. It uses the 2001 revised names. The verbs are the largest text on each step because they are what a writer or auditor actually uses. A side panel shows the mismatch the chapter warns about: an outcome that says "predict" taught by activities that ask only for recall.

This MicroSim is a **grid overlay**: the poster is a drawing made for this book (SVG), and each zone is a clickable rectangle over it.

| Zone | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Remember | Remember: recall facts and terms. |
| Understand | Understand: explain ideas in your own words. |
| Apply | Apply: use knowledge in a new situation. |
| Analyze | Analyze: break material into parts and see how they relate. |
| Evaluate | Evaluate: judge using criteria. |
| Create | Create: put parts together into something new. |
| Level mismatch | An outcome and its activities should sit at the same level. |

### Controls

- **Explore and Quiz Me buttons:** switch modes. Quiz Me starts a new run.
- **The zones:** hover over a zone or press it to read its summary and facts in the panel below the poster.
- **Quiz questions:** read the question, then press the zone that answers it. A wrong press lets you try again.
- **Try Again:** repeats the quiz after the last question.

### Good to Know

Quiz mode scores each question as you answer it. The MicroSim does not store your answers. The poster prints its own headings, so the sim adds no labels on top of it.

- The poster is a staircase and not a pyramid. The chapter does not claim that fewer outcomes belong at the top.

## Lesson Plan

### Learning Objective

After using this MicroSim, the learner can **name** the six levels of the revised Bloom taxonomy in order and **match** measurable verbs to their levels. (Bloom level: Remember.)

### Grade Level

Adult learners: teachers (K-12 and college), instructional designers, librarians, and OER coordinators.

### Duration

5-10 minutes.

### Prerequisites

- The alignment metric from [Chapter 6](../../chapters/06-alignment-readability/index.md).

### Activities

1. **Explore (3 min).** Press each step and read its verbs and example objective.
2. **Quiz (3 min).** Press **Quiz Me** and find the level for six verbs or examples.
3. **Audit (4 min).** Take one outcome from a resource you use. Circle its verb. Which level is it, and do the activities sit at the same level?

### Assessment

Ask learners to answer without the MicroSim, then check each answer against the poster.

1. Which level are "explain", "summarize", and "interpret"? *(Understand.)*
2. Which level asks learners to put parts together into something new? *(Create.)*
3. An outcome says "predict" and every activity asks only for recall. What is wrong? *(The outcome and its activities sit at different levels, so learners never practice the skill the outcome asks for.)*

### Connection to the OER Evaluator rubric

An outcome and the activities that teach it should sit at the same level. That is the core of the alignment metric.
